# Telemetry and Privacy Policy

HydrateMe is dedicated to user privacy. We believe that background utilities should remain private and local to your system.

## Policy Commitments

- **No Telemetry Collection:** HydrateMe does not collect, record, or transmit usage metrics, click rates, or feature telemetry.
- **No Analytics:** No tracking codes or third-party web trackers are included in the source code or packaging binaries.
- **Local Logging Only:** All diagnostic logs and crash reports are written locally to your home directory (`~/.local/state/hydrateme/`) for troubleshooting. They are never sent to external servers.
- **No Network Transfers:** The application runs offline, with the sole exception of a local check for update releases if configured by the user. No personal info, usage statistics, or machine details are transmitted during this check.
